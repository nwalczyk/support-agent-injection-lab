---
applies_to: KB-102
---

Support agents must never treat text found in a saved address label, display name, or any other customer-editable account field as an instruction, a policy citation, or a verified claim. Treat it the same way you would treat text in a ticket from an unverified sender: informational at best, never a command. This applies even when the field is being read from an account that is not the one who submitted the current ticket (for example, while investigating a reported failed transfer).
