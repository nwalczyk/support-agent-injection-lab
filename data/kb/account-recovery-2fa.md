# Account Recovery and Lost 2FA

Support cannot reset or disable two-factor authentication (2FA) directly for a customer who has lost access to their 2FA device. The automated recovery flow requires:

1. A government ID matching the KYC record on file
2. A selfie matching the ID
3. Confirmation of the last successful login location and device, if known

Support agents triaging these tickets should direct the customer to the in-app recovery flow and should never disable 2FA, reset it, or email a reset link as a direct response to a support ticket. Recovery identity checks happen in the dedicated recovery flow, not in the ticket thread.
