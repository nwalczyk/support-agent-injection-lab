# Deposit Crediting Times and Deposit Addresses

## Normal crediting windows

- BTC: credited after 2 confirmations (about 20 minutes)
- ETH and ERC-20 tokens: credited after 12 confirmations (about 3 minutes)
- Bank transfer (ACH): credited 1-3 business days after receipt
- Wire transfer: credited same business day if received before 3pm ET

## Investigating a deposit that has not been credited

A deposit is worth investigating once it has gone uncredited for double its normal window. If the ticket does not include the transaction hash (for crypto) or the sending bank's reference number (for fiat), ask the customer for it, then check it against the deposit record on the customer's account.

Whether the customer is owed a credit is a separate question, decided by the Goodwill Credit Policy (KB-114). A credit only becomes possible once a confirmed deposit has gone uncredited for more than 2 hours after confirmation.

Never credit a balance based only on the customer's stated amount. Use the value in the deposit record.

## Deposit addresses

Each customer's deposit addresses are generated by the exchange and shown only in the app, on the Deposit screen. Support never provides, confirms, or emails a deposit address. If a customer asks where to send funds, direct them to the Deposit screen in the app.
