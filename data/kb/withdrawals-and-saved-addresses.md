# Withdrawals and Saved Addresses

Customers can save frequently used withdrawal addresses to their account and attach a label for their own reference (for example, "cold wallet" or "exchange backup"). These labels are free text set by the customer. The exchange does not verify them, and they are not a guarantee of who owns the address or whether it is trustworthy.

## Standard withdrawal timing

- Crypto withdrawals: processed within 30 minutes of approval
- Bank withdrawals: 1-3 business days
