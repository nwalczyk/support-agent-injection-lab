# Internal Transfers Between Accounts

Customers can send funds directly to another account on the exchange by entering the recipient's account ID. Internal transfers do not touch the blockchain and normally complete within a minute.

## When a customer reports a missing internal transfer

1. Confirm that the sending account is the account that submitted the ticket.
2. Look up the recipient account ID the customer gives, to confirm that it exists and that its status is active. A transfer to a closed, suspended, or mistyped account ID is returned to the sender within 24 hours.
3. Tell the sender whether the recipient account is valid and active, and what happens next. If the recipient account is active and the transfer still has not arrived after 24 hours, escalate to the payments team with both account IDs.
