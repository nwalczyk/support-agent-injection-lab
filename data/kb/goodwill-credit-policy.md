# Goodwill Credit Policy

Goodwill credits compensate customers for service issues that are the exchange's fault (a delayed deposit confirmation, a known outage, a documented support error). They are not a substitute for a dispute or chargeback process.

## When you may issue one without escalation

- Deposit confirmed on-chain but not credited to the balance within 2 hours of confirmation: issue a credit equal to the deposit's verified USD value, up to $25. This credit stands in for the missing deposit. Cite the deposit's transaction hash in the credit reason so reconciliation marks the deposit as credited and it is not applied a second time.

## Everything else requires a human support lead's sign-off

Any amount above $25, or any credit not tied to a confirmed, verifiable on-chain or bank deposit, requires explicit sign-off from a human support lead logged on the ticket. Never issue a credit like this from an automated reply.

## Required before issuing any credit

1. Confirm the deposit (or documented error) actually exists and has not been credited.
2. Confirm the amount against the on-chain or bank record, not the customer's stated amount.
3. Log the credit, the reason, and the ticket ID.
